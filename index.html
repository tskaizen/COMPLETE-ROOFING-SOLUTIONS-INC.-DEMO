What goes into a Florida roof

The parts you never see are the parts that keep water out

Palm Beach County roofs have to stand up to hurricane winds, summer storms and salt air. Every layer matters.

  • Finished roofTile, shingle, metal or a flat roof membrane, fastened to Florida Building Code.
  • UnderlaymentThe water barrier under the finished roof. This is what stops leaks when wind lifts a tile.
  • Roof deckPlywood sheathing. Soft or rotted sheets get replaced during a reroof.
  • TrussesThe frame of the roof. We check connections and report anything we find.
