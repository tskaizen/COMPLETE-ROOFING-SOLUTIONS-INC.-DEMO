Our story

Roofing is the family business

Complete Roofing Solutions has served South Florida since 1985. The company is led by owner George Jacobazzi, who served as 2024 President of the Palm Beach County Roofing and Sheet Metal Contractors Association.

Our crews handle residential and commercial roofing, repairs, replacements and maintenance. No project is too big or too small, and every job gets the same attention to the details that keep water out.
