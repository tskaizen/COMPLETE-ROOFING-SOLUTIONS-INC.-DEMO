Questions we hear a lot

Which roof is right for my house?

It depends on your budget, how long you plan to stay and your HOA rules. Tile is the most common here, shingle costs the least up front, and metal lasts a long time. We walk you through each during the inspection.

Do I need an inspection before I get a price?

You can see a ballpark range right now with our instant estimate. The exact price comes after we measure the roof and check the deck.

Do you work with HOAs?

Yes. We can provide product information and samples so your HOA can approve the material and color.
