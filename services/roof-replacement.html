Questions we hear a lot

How do I know it is time to replace?

Repeated leaks, cracked or slipping tile, curling shingles and soft spots on the deck are the usual signs. An inspection tells you whether a repair will buy more time.

How long does a reroof take?

Most homes take a few days on the roof once the permit is issued. Tile jobs take longer than shingle because of the weight and detail work.

Can you go over my old roof?

Florida code limits how many layers a roof can have, and tile always comes off. We recommend a full tear off so the deck can be inspected.
