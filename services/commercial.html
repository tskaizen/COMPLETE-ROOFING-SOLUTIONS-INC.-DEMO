Questions we hear a lot

Do you work with property managers and boards?

Yes. We provide written proposals, product data and schedules that are easy to take to a board meeting.

Can you coat our roof instead of replacing it?

If the existing roof is dry and sound, a coating can add years for less money. A core sample and inspection tell us if it qualifies.

Can you work while the building stays open?

In most cases, yes. We plan staging, access and noise around your tenants.
